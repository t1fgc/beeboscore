# Running a local, start to finish

[← BeeboScore guide](../GUIDE.md)

A walkthrough of a weekly — from opening BeeboScore to posting the results — using the start.gg event, the live bracket page for players and the end-of-night standings. Every step links to the page with the details.

## Before people arrive (10 minutes)

1. **Open BeeboScore and OBS.** The Matches tab warns you if OBS isn't showing the overlay. ([Install & OBS](install-and-obs.md))
2. **Pick the game.** The game menu at the top (or Matches › Game) — its Classic layout follows. ([Layouts](layouts.md))
3. **Load the event.** Bracket tab › **Sign in with start.gg**, then pick your tournament under **My tournaments** and press **Pull players**. ([Brackets](brackets.md))
4. **Turn on the live bracket page** (Bracket tab › Share & export). Post the link in your Discord or show it on a screen at the venue — players type their name and see who they play next. If you're streaming, **Go online** first (Stream tab) so people outside the venue can open it. ([Going online](online.md))
5. **Check the sound and the Stream Deck.** Settings › Sound (off until you turn it on), and the Stream Deck keys you use most: score, bracket splash, Top 8, standings. ([Stream Deck](stream-deck.md))
6. **Busy venue Wi-Fi?** Leave *Let phones and tablets on my Wi-Fi control without the password* off, and give your helpers the remote-control password instead. ([Going online › Security](online.md#security))

## During the bracket

1. **Load a match:** Bracket tab › the match list › **Load** (or the start.gg stream queue). Both players and the round name fill in. ([Running matches](matches.md))
2. **Score it** with + / −, the shortcuts or the Stream Deck. When someone wins, the victory splash comes up by itself.
3. **Between sets:** show the bracket (**Show bracket**) — it switches to the **Top 8** by itself once the Top 8 is set.

## At the end of the night

1. When the last match is confirmed, the **Top 8 standings** come up on stream and are saved as pictures (16:9 and square) in the tournament's folder — **Open folder**.
2. **Copy post text** and post it with the picture.
3. The live bracket link closes by itself 15 minutes later; the pictures stay until you delete or replace the tournament (at most 24 hours), so save them somewhere else.
4. Your setup is backed up by itself once a day (Settings › Backups). ([Settings](settings.md))
