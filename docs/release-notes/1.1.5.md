## BeeboScore 1.1.5

Already on an older version? Open **Settings → Check for updates** in the app and it will fetch this one for you.

### Improved

- **A starting screen.** A small "BeeboScore: starting up" picture now shows while the exe unpacks itself, so you can see it is on its way. It also shows after **Restart & update**, where there used to be several seconds with nothing on screen.
- **Restart & update says what it is doing.** Before the window closes, the app tells you it is installing the new version and that it will open again by itself.
- **A safer hand-over** between the old version and the new one.
- **What's new** lists every version you skipped.

Updating from 1.0.0 or 1.1.4: the window still closes without a message, because that part belongs to the version you are leaving. The starting screen appears about a second later. From 1.1.5 on, you get both.

New in 1.1.4, if you are coming from 1.0.0: ten new layouts, your own VS Splash pictures, splash pictures per player and more. The What's new window lists them all after you update.

### Download

**`BeeboScore-1.1.5.exe`** below. No install: put it in the same folder as your current exe so it keeps your settings.
