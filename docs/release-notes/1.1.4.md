## BeeboScore 1.1.4

Already on 1.0.0? Open **Settings → Check for updates** in the app and it will fetch this version for you.

### New

- **Ten new layouts.** The Layout menu now has 11: **Ember** (the original, called Shattered until now), **Frostbite**, **Venom**, **Royal**, **Crimson**, **Gold Rush**, **Sakura**, **Tidal**, **Voltage**, **Chrome** and **Pastel**.
  - Each has its own bar shape, lettering, effects and animations, its own VS Splash and its own Victory Splash.
  - All of them sit in the same place over the game as Ember.
  - In every layout the score is in a solid, high-contrast box, and stays readable for the player who lost.
  - They never draw over the game's health bars, round counters, timer or portraits. Only the short burst when a game or a set is won goes past the bar.
- **Your own VS Splash pictures.** In the VS Splash card on the Matches tab, add a picture for the bar behind each player's name, for a header across the top, or both. They show in every layout.
  - Best sizes: name bar **920 × 250**, header **1920 × 200**. PNG, WebP or JPG. Other sizes are scaled and cropped to fit.
  - **Lettering matched to your picture.** The app picks light or dark names, a tint of the picture's colour for the team tag, and a soft backing if the picture is busy, so the names don't blend in. **Pick my own** lets you choose the colours and warns you if they are hard to read.
- **Splash pictures.** On the start.gg tab, choose what each player shows on the VS Splash and Victory Splash: their start.gg profile picture, or a character image you upload for them.
  - Best size: **900 × 1080** (a tall picture). PNG, WebP or JPG.
  - **Crop and green screen.** After you choose a picture, a window opens where you can crop it and remove a green-screen background before it is saved.
  - **Find a player** completes names as you type, so the card stays short for an event of any size.
- **VS Splash button** on the Matches tab (or press Ctrl + I).
- **Victory Splash** shows the winner's character image as a large panel when they use one.
- **Stream Deck: VS Splash key.** Install the updated plugin from **File → Install Stream Deck Plugin**.
- **What's new window.** Opens once after you update and lists what changed. Settings has a button to open it again.

### Changed

- Shattered is now called **Ember**. It is the same layout.
- Ember's Victory Splash no longer has crack lines behind "Victory".

### Easier to read (Ember)

- The loser's final score and name stay readable after the plate cracks.
- The winner's name stands out from the lit plate.
- The small lettering on the scoreboard is larger and brighter.

### Fixed

- VS Splash: names are centred under a character image, the last letter of a slanted name is no longer cut off, and a short name keeps its size next to a very long one.
- Very long event and opponent names are shortened with "…" instead of running into other text.
- The saved-layout list in the Overlay maker refreshes before "Saved" is shown.
