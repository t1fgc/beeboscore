## BeeboScore 1.4.0

The biggest update since 1.0.0: game formats, a new layout, a Stream Deck that sets itself up, and a lighter overlay.

Already on 1.1.8? Open **Settings → Check for updates** in the app and it will fetch this one for you.

### New: game formats

A new **Formats** tab runs seven kinds of event on top of the scoreboard. The format decides who plays next; you score each set as usual and press **Confirm & next match**.

- **Crew Battle:** winner stays on.
- **Team Battle** (Sajam Slam style): players paired by tier, first team to the target points.
- **Gauntlet:** one player against a line of challengers.
- **Team Death Battle:** two teams, every fighter has lives, and a team can switch fighters after a life is lost. A fighter who comes back still has the lives they left with.
- **Death Battle:** everyone has lives; the last one standing wins.
- **King of the Hill:** winner stays, loser goes to the back of the queue.
- **League:** everyone plays everyone.

Both layouts draw every format in their own style: lives, crowns and tiers on the player bars, a format bar along the bottom, Board, Team VS and Champion splashes, and moments such as a lost life, a switch or a knockout. Nothing changes in OBS.

- **Lock-in splash.** In team formats, when both players are locked in, two halves of glass slide together with each player's picture, name and lives or points, meld, and a light runs down the join.
- **Player pictures.** Drop any picture on a player in the Formats tab. A big one opens a crop window first, then it is made smaller for you and used on their card and on the splashes. Players without a picture get their name and a big letter on a card the same size.
- **Run it from the Matches tab:** a console with the matchup, Confirm & next match, Forfeit, Undo, the On stream buttons and, for Team Death Battle, the switch window.

The full guide: [docs/FORMATS.md](https://github.com/t1fgc/beeboscore/blob/main/docs/FORMATS.md)

### New layout: Porcelain

Ember glazed white, with dark lettering and ember-orange fire. When a set ends the loser's bar shatters and is knocked out of shape. It has its own VS Splash, Victory Splash and format pieces.

### Stream Deck

- **Set up my Stream Deck** (Settings → Stream Deck): one press puts every key on your 15-key Stream Deck or Stream Deck XL, sorted, with keys in the middle that change with the format. On Windows, Stream Deck closes for a few seconds and comes back with a new page; your other pages stay.
- **A new plugin, "BeeboScore Prototype"**, with new key pictures that are easy to tell apart, and fewer keys that each do more.
- **Refresh OBS**, in the header and on the Stream Deck, reloads the overlay in OBS without a visible reload.

### Also new

- **Settings → Scoreboard:** choose whether the Victory Splash plays by itself after a set, how long it stays up, and whether the lock-in splash plays.
- **Seamless reset:** Reset and Clear play the bar's own way back before the overlay refreshes.

### Changed

- **The prototype notice is gone** from the top of the app.
- **Two layouts: Ember and Porcelain.** The ten elemental layouts (Frostbite, Venom, Royal, Crimson, Gold Rush, Sakura, Tidal, Voltage, Chrome and Pastel) are out for now. They are being reworked and will come back later.
- **Clear of the timer:** Ember's middle tag is shorter, and the player bars wrap round the round-win circles with a lit rim for each.
- The fighter playing has a **bigger card**, a fighter on their **last life** gets a heartbeat, and **switching fighters** has its own animation.

### Fixed and faster

- Moments and splashes no longer land on top of each other.
- Names on crowded bars and knocked-out pieces are shown whole.
- The overlay's effects are drawn in a cheaper way: about half the work each second in OBS.

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/t1fgc/beeboscore/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

### Updating from 1.1.8

- Your settings, players, start.gg sign-in and overlays carry over.
- **Used one of the ten elemental layouts?** The app switches you to Ember. If you had edited one, your copy is kept in `BeeboScore Data\data\retired-layouts`.
- **Stream Deck:** open **Settings → Stream Deck**, press **Install** for the new plugin, then **Set up my Stream Deck**. You can then remove the old *BeeboScore* plugin in Stream Deck (right-click it in the list → Uninstall).
